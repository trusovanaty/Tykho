One blue, many quiet steps

A single hue in tonal steps plus ink. The solid blue appears once per screen — the answer, or what needs you. Selection is a thin blue ring, not a fill. Blue is among the most preferred colours for children with ASD; yellow and saturated warm tones are avoided entirely.

07 · Rules

Do / Don't

Do
  • One solid blue block per screen — the answer or what needs you
  • Selection = thin blue ring + soft fill + check
  • Pills for actions, circles for icons & dates
  • Light 300 for big text, generous air
  • Glass only over the sky gradient
  • Every state: shape + colour + word
Don't
  • Second hue, yellow, saturated warm tones
  • Big blue fills, stacked solid blocks
  • Borders on cards, hard shadows
  • Emoji, cartoons, puzzle pieces, medical imagery
  • Bounce, confetti, pulsing, auto-carousels
  • Text below 15px (chips excepted)
